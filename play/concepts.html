TEWGOLAB

Five live concepts for the strip above the board and the section below it. Everything here is drawn with the real game art. Poke each one, then pick a letter or mix pieces of several.

CONCEPT A, TOP STRIP

Capture shelves

Instead of "You 2/5", every captured pair sits on your shelf as the actual enemy figures you took. Five filled sockets is the win, so the shelf itself shows how close each side is.

In the real game the pair would fly from the board onto the shelf.

CONCEPT B, LOWER SECTION

Face-off loadout

Your figure and your opponent's figure are the controls. Tap either one to change it, exactly like picking a fighter. The pickers you have today open from here, so the six dropdowns disappear.

VS
Space

Try tapping the two figures. They cycle through the Space roster.

CONCEPT C, LOWER SECTION

Theme shelf

All eight worlds on display, like a trophy shelf. Locked worlds stay visible in silhouette so players can see what they are playing toward. The thresholds below are the real ones from the iOS app. Drag the slider to watch worlds unlock.

Games finished: 2

Space and Feudal Japan start unlocked. Dungeon opens at 3 games, Ocean at 7, Undead at 12, Western at 20, Desert at 30, Classic at 38.

CONCEPT D, LOWER SECTION

Profile dock

The page becomes THEIR page. Their figure is their avatar, their name sits next to their vs AI record, and the gold bar shows progress toward the Gold color unlock in the current world.

CONCEPT E, LOWER SECTION

Profile dock plus theme medallions

The combination I would ship. The dock from D is the always-on bar, and a row of world medallions sits under it. Tap an unlocked medallion to jump worlds. Tap the shelf button to expand the full theme shelf from C.
