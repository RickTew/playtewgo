TEWGO

Features and updates

What TEWGO has today

The game

  • A 22x22 board and two ways to win: five in a row, or capture five pairs of your opponent's pieces.
  • Every move attacks and defends at once. Flank two enemy pieces to capture them.
  • Pinch to zoom and pan the board; tap to place.

Ways to play

  • Play vs AI on four difficulty levels: Easy, Medium, Hard, Expert. Expert reads your best reply before it commits, so it sees the traps.
  • Pass & Play with a friend on one device.
  • Play online through Game Center: real-time or turn-based matches.
  • Match by anything for the fastest game, or match by your theme for identical worlds.

Make it yours

  • Eight themed worlds: Space, Feudal Japan, Dungeon, Ocean, Undead, Western, Desert, Classic.
  • You see the whole game in your own theme, board, pieces, and music, even against an opponent playing in a different world.
  • Pick any piece from a theme's roster for each side, plus one of 12 color combos including the dark Midnight and Forest pairs.
  • Four piece types (Flat, Chip, Half, Tall) and two finishes (Standard, 3D).
  • 12 or more background scenes per theme, four neutral backdrops, and board surfaces you can layer over any background: 7 universal materials plus 4 themed boards per world.
  • Five audio tracks per theme, from menu music to capture effects.

Earn it by playing

  • Everything unlocks by playing, never by paying to win. Two themes are free; the rest unlock as you play.
  • The rare Gold finish is earned with wins in each theme.
  • Victory share card after a win, and a trophy room in your profile.
  • An optional one-time Unlock All purchase ($2.59, and the 2 and 5 are a nod to the name: TEW = 2, GO = 5) for people who want everything now. It never changes gameplay.

The free browser game

  • playtewgo.com/play: free, no account, works great on Android phones.
  • Same rules engine as the iOS app, ported test for test.
  • Play vs AI or two players on one screen. All eight themed worlds with the full set of scenes, boards, pieces, and audio.
  • The same four AI difficulty levels as iOS, Expert included. The AI defends by capturing when that is the better save, and it protects its own pairs instead of gifting you captures.
  • Same unlock ladder as iOS: Space and Feudal Japan are free, and the other six open as you finish games. Finishing counts whether you win or lose.
  • Your own profile: name yourself, your figure becomes your avatar, and your collection is counted for you across worlds, figures, backgrounds, and soundtracks.
  • Captured pairs appear above the board as the actual pieces you took, not a score.
  • Your game auto-saves in the browser, and you can install it like an app.
  • Win-based progression: your vs AI record is kept on your device, and 10 wins in a theme unlock its rare Gold colors, same rule as iOS.
  • An optional one-time Unlock All Worlds purchase ($2.59), the same price and the same cosmetic-only promise as the iOS one. Restore anywhere with your TEWGO code.

Updates

1.0 Live

  • The launch release, July 2026: the full game above on iPhone and iPad.

1.0.1 Live

  • A brand new app icon.
  • 3D piece finish: pick Standard or 3D in the piece picker and watch your stones sit on the board.
  • Midnight and Forest dark color schemes for late-night games.

1.0.2 Live

  • A new Expert AI difficulty: it reads your best answer before committing, so it spots the traps and refuses the bait.
  • Show off your style online: opponents see the piece and finish you picked, your pieces in gold when you share a theme, and a sparkle by your name everywhere else.
  • Report a Problem in Settings: writes us an email with your version and device already filled in.

1.0.3 Live

  • AI tuning: the AI values its pairs properly and no longer hands over free captures.
  • Smarter defense: Hard and Expert block a four for good instead of trading a capture you can simply rebuild, but still capture when it truly kills the line or wins the pair race.
  • The difficulty ladder, rebuilt: each step up now plays like a genuinely different opponent, not just a stronger one. Easy shows you a capture once and then stops taking freebies.
  • "Flanked!" - the first time your pair gets captured, the game tells you what happened right where the stones vanished. And game over now shows the pair race next to the win reason.
  • Your profile keeps a record for each AI level, and the piece that moved last is gently highlighted.

1.0.4 Live

  • The AI plays the capture race: it takes the pairs you leave on offer instead of walking past them, and how hungry it is for the race is now part of the difficulty. Easy still just shows you one capture; Expert races you for five pairs.
  • The winner banner announces and then steps aside, so you can see the board it was covering, and a five-in-a-row win lights up the exact five stones that won it.
  • The "Flanked!" callout stays a readable size however far you have zoomed in.

Next up In development

  • New ways to earn and show off as you play.
  • The AI defends its own pairs. It would take a pair of yours, but walk away from one of its own that you were one move from capturing. Saving a pair was scored at a quarter of what it costs to leave one hanging; it is worth half now, and Expert gives away barely more than half as many pairs a game. Already live in the browser game.
  • A bigger "Flanked!" callout that stays up long enough to read, even when you were the one who captured and the AI answers straight away.
  • Review your game: after any game ends, step back through it move by move and find the moment it turned. Already live in the browser game.
  • Browser game: online play.
