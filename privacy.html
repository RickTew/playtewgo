TEWGO Privacy Policy

Last updated: July 11, 2026

The short version

TEWGO does not collect, store, or share any personal data. There are no accounts, no analytics, no ads, and no tracking.

What stays on your device

Your game settings and progress (chosen themes, pieces, unlocks, win counts) are stored only on your device using Apple's standard app storage. They are never sent to us or to anyone else.

Game Center

Online multiplayer uses Apple's Game Center. When you play online, Apple handles matchmaking and player identity under Apple's own privacy policy. TEWGO only sees the Game Center display name and moves needed to run the match, and does not store them anywhere outside the match itself.

In-app purchases

Purchases are processed entirely by Apple's App Store. We never see your payment details.

Children

TEWGO collects no data from anyone, including children.

Changes

If this policy ever changes, the new version will be posted at this address with an updated date.
