## Ranked findings (by persona count, then severity)

### 1. The rules live in the footer; the first game teaches the wrong model (5/5 personas)
Maya 1+8, Leo 3, Marcus 1, Priya 1, Frank 1+4. The only rules text
("Five in a row wins. Capturing five pairs also wins. Flank two enemy
pieces to capture them.") sits below the fold in small grey type. Every
persona started game 1 blind; Maya and Priya finished game 1 believing
the game is plain five-in-a-row; the "0/5" trays read as broken UI until
the first capture. FIX (web UI): surface the one-liner above the board
for a profile with 0 games played, and label the trays ("pairs").

### 2. Captures are invisible in the moment they happen (4/5, incl. a repaint bug)
Maya 6 (severity: would-quit - "is this a bug? did it just cheat?"),
Leo 4, Marcus 3, Frank praised the shelf but not the moment. **Leo's
repaint bug is the hard finding: captured stones remained VISIBLY
STANDING on the board for over half a minute after the capture (verified
by screenshot comparison), while the tray and counter had already
updated.** A player plans around stones that are already dead. FIX
(web): captured pair must leave the board the same frame the counter
ticks, with a visible effect (the "beam-up" Leo asked for); plus a
one-time first-capture callout pointing at the tray (Maya's fix).

### 3. World locks are not enforced on web; the $2.59 product is undercut (3/5, would-quit for monetization)
Marcus 6 (selected "Ocean (4 more games)" in the New game dialog, hit
Start, played a full Ocean game), Priya 11 (below-board theme dropdown
lists all 8 worlds with no lock marks; switched into locked Western
mid-game), Maya 13 (setup dropdown options ARE disabled for real
clicks, but the page script itself does not re-check on change - so at
least one real-click path plus the programmatic path both bypass).
NOT [INTENDED]: PLAYTEST NOTES say the unlock ladder is deliberate, and
iOS enforces it. Priya: "nobody pays to skip a fence with an open
gate." FIX (web): enforce the lock in game-start/theme-change logic
itself, not just in dropdown option state; add lock marks to the
below-board dropdown.

### 4. One tap on "Unlock all worlds $2.59" lands on a live Stripe card page (3/5)
Leo 7 (would-quit: "a kid reached a real payment form in one click"),
Frank 7, Marcus 7. Same tab, game replaced, no confirmation step.
Return path with "Continue game" works (praised by all three). FIX
(web): an in-game confirm step ("$2.59, one time, cosmetic only -
Continue to payment?") between the button and Stripe.

### 5. Game-over screen does not show the winning line legibly (3/5)
Priya 4 (borderline would-quit: reconstructed Expert's hidden split-four
by hand from screenshots), Maya 5, Marcus (game 3). Frank saw a glowing
line in game 1, so a highlight EXISTS but is dimmed/illegible or absent
for some outcomes. FIX (web): highlight the winning five (or the fifth
captured pair) bright, over an undimmed board, until dismissed.

### 6. Engine: the AI leaks pairs and ignores its own captures (2/5, the two strongest players)
Frank 5 ("the biggest one for me"): farmed Expert 5 pairs to 2 on his
FIRST attempt - it hung diagonal pairs onto his waiting flanks
repeatedly, and sat on three or four one-move captures for several
turns while Frank was one pair from a capture win. Priya 5: Medium,
Hard, and Expert all voluntarily built the same capturable pair early
and ignored her flank threat for two turns - "a repeatable free capture
recipe." NOT [INTENDED] - this is the same weakness class Rick has
reported from iOS three times. FIX (engine, both repos): raise the base
vulnerable-pair penalty so shape-building never parks a pair cheaply,
and add capture urgency when the opponent is close to a capture win.
Routed to the iOS session (engine source of truth).
