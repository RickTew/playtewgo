How it works

Two ways to win

Line up five of your pieces in a row, or capture five pairs of your opponent's. Every move attacks and defends at once.

Play anyone

A friend on the same device, players online in real-time or turn-based matches through Game Center, or an AI with three difficulty levels.

Make it yours

Space, Feudal Japan, Dungeon, Ocean, Undead, Western, Desert, Classic. Your board, your pieces, your music. Everything unlocks by playing, never by paying to win.

Play in your browser

Play TEWGO right now, free in your browser.

Same rules as the iOS game. Face the AI on three difficulty levels. Works great on Android phones.
