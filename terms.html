Your progress lives on your device

This is the most important practical thing on this page, so it gets its own section.

The browser game has no accounts and no cloud save. Your record, your unlocked worlds, your settings and your game in progress are stored in your own browser and nowhere else. That means all of the following will lose your progress, and none of them are recoverable by me:

  • Clearing your browsing data or site data for playtewgo.com.
  • Playing in a private or incognito window and then closing it.
  • Switching to a different browser, or to a different device.
  • A browser cleaning up storage on its own, which some do when disk space runs low.

Your unlock is the one part that survives all of this, because you have the code. Everything else is a game record, so please treat it as a game record and not as something irreplaceable.
